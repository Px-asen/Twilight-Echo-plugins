# User-supplied navigation artwork

The 30 PNG images were supplied by the user in two local asset packs:

- `TwilightEcho_Miku_Nav_Icons`, under `transparent_512/`: 13 main navigation icons.
- `TwilightEcho_Miku_Settings_Streaming_Icons`, under
  `settings_nav/transparent_512/` and `streaming_nav/transparent_512/`:
  9 settings navigation icons and 8 streaming navigation icons.

The supplied README files describe them as transparent crops of generated icon
sheets. No separate artwork author or redistribution license was supplied.

For this preview, the images were resized from 512×512 to 96×96 pixels with their
alpha channels preserved. The character designs and artwork are not relicensed
under the surrounding source repository's Apache-2.0 license.

## User-supplied homepage illustration

`artwork/miku-home.jpg` is the original 1969×1599 JPEG supplied by the user for
the homepage card. Its bytes are preserved; cropping, fading and dark appearance
are applied by CSS at display time. No author or redistribution license was
supplied. The illustration is included for the requested local preview and is
not relicensed under the surrounding repository's license.

## User-supplied song-list background

`artwork/miku-sky.webp` derives from the user-supplied `149088559_p0.jpg`
(2160×3840). It is proportionally resized to 1215×2160 and WebP-encoded at
quality 76 for CSS delivery. No compositional crop or character repaint is baked
into this resource. The original remains in the user's picture folder.
The visible signature in the source is retained in the full resource; display
cropping depends on window shape. No separate redistribution license was supplied.
This local preview does not relicense the illustration.

## User-supplied bouquet global background

`artwork/miku-bouquet.webp` derives from the user-supplied `101123738_p0.jpg`
(5262×3672), proportionally resized to 2560 pixels wide and encoded as WebP at
quality 82. The original remains unchanged in the user's picture folder; viewport
cropping and masks are applied by CSS. The supplied author list does not map
individual filenames to artists. No separate redistribution license was supplied.
This local preview does not relicense the illustration.

## User-supplied chibi state illustrations

Each 1254×1254 RGBA source is proportionally resized to 384×384 and lossless WebP encoded with transparency preserved. No separate author or redistribution license was supplied.
